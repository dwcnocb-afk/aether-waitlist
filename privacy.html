Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

This policy explains how Aether AI collects and uses information when you visit this website or join the Aether AI founding-member waitlist.

Who we are and what this policy covers

“AETHER AI,” “we,” or “us” refers to the AETHER AI business operated from Nigeria, which determines why and how the waitlist information described here is used. Contact us at dwcnocb@gmail.com. This policy covers this marketing website and the broker waitlist only. The waitlist form is for mortgage professionals interested in Aether AI. Do not submit borrower names, loan information, financial details, or other borrower data through it.

This notice does not describe how a broker's borrower information may be handled if that information is later processed by the Aether AI product. That processing would require separate product terms and privacy information.

Information we collect

  • Information you submit: full name, work email address, brokerage name, and (optionally) team size.
  • Waitlist record details: the date and time you joined, your consent to be contacted about the waitlist and early access, signup source, and waitlist status.
  • Technical information: our Vercel hosting and serverless function provider may process technical request and log information such as IP address, browser or device details, timestamps, and diagnostics to deliver, maintain, and secure the site. The information and retention involved depend on provider settings and their service terms.

We do not request sensitive personal information or borrower information in the waitlist form.

How we use information

We use your submission and consent to record your interest, manage the waitlist, assess whether AETHER AI may fit your brokerage, and contact you only about the waitlist and early access. We use limited technical information to operate, troubleshoot, and protect the website, and use or disclose information when required by law. You can withdraw consent at any time by emailing us; withdrawal does not affect processing that occurred before withdrawal.

Joining the waitlist does not subscribe you to unrelated promotional mailings. We do not use waitlist information for targeted advertising, and we do not sell it. Waitlist details are not used to make lending decisions or to score borrowers.

Legal basis

For waitlist details, we rely on your affirmative consent to manage your place on the list and contact you about early access. You may withdraw consent at any time by emailing us. For limited technical information needed to operate, troubleshoot, and protect the site, we rely on those purposes only where a lawful basis is available under the law that applies. We may also retain or disclose information when required to meet a legal obligation. You may decline to provide optional details; without your name and email address, we cannot manage your waitlist entry or contact you.

Who may receive information

We use service providers to operate the website and waitlist:

  • Vercel hosts the website and runs the signup endpoint. See the Vercel Privacy Notice.
  • Airtable stores waitlist records in a separate waitlist base. See the Airtable Privacy Policy.
  • Calendly is linked for people who choose to book a conversation. If you follow that link and enter information there, Calendly handles that information under its own Privacy Notice.

We may also disclose information when necessary to comply with law, protect rights or safety, or respond to a valid legal request. We do not authorize our service providers to use waitlist records for their own unrelated marketing.

Retention

We keep waitlist contact details only while the waitlist remains active and the information is still needed to administer it and send the updates described above. We will review whether the waitlist is still active at least once every 12 months. If we close or discontinue it, we will delete or anonymize waitlist contact details within 90 days, unless we need specific information for a legal obligation, dispute, or to document a privacy request or consent withdrawal. You may request deletion sooner at any time. Limited provider logs and backups may persist until they expire under the provider's retention schedule.

Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on the law that applies, you may ask us to confirm whether we process your personal data and access it; correct inaccurate details; delete it; receive a copy in a portable format; restrict or object to processing; or withdraw consent. You may also complain to a data protection authority. We will verify requests reasonably and respond within applicable legal time limits. Send requests to dwcnocb@gmail.com. If we decline a request and the applicable law gives you a right to appeal, you may ask for a review by replying to our decision.

We do not sell personal information, use it for targeted advertising, or make decisions about you using profiling or solely automated processing. If Texas Chapter 541 applies to our processing, its rights may include access, correction, deletion, a portable copy, and opting out of sale, targeted advertising, or certain profiling. We do not currently engage in those opt-out activities. You do not need an account to make a privacy request. If you are in Nigeria and believe we have not handled a request appropriately, you may also contact the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. Texas rights and duties depend on whether Chapter 541 applies to the business and processing in question, including its scope and exemptions. See Texas Business & Commerce Code, Chapter 541.

Cookies and analytics

The current landing-page code does not add analytics or advertising trackers. Vercel may process technical logs needed to operate and secure the site. If we add analytics, advertising, or other tracking technologies, we will update this policy and provide choices or notices where required.

International processing

AETHER AI is operated from Nigeria and serves brokers in the United States. Waitlist information is stored in Airtable and the site is hosted on Vercel. These providers and their subprocessors may process information in the United States and other countries where they operate. Nigeria's Data Protection Act sets conditions for transfers from Nigeria, including an adequate protection mechanism or a specific statutory exception, and requires the transfer basis and adequacy to be recorded. The particular transfer route and safeguards depend on the provider terms and arrangements applicable to our accounts. You may contact us for further information. We will confirm and document an applicable transfer basis before accepting live waitlist submissions.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures intended to protect waitlist information. The signup endpoint is designed to keep Airtable credentials on the server rather than in browser code; access to stored records is also subject to the permissions configured for the Airtable base. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children

This website and waitlist are intended for mortgage professionals and are not directed to children. Please do not use the waitlist if you are under 18. We do not knowingly collect children's information through it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our practices or legal requirements change. We will post the revised version here and update the effective date. Please review this page periodically.

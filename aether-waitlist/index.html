The problem

It's hard to move fast when every lead lands in a different place.

A busy day can turn a new enquiry into one more tab, inbox thread, or sticky note. Then the question isn't just “Did I reply?” It's “Who should I call next?”

A simpler lead flow

From new enquiry to a clear next move.

Aether AI brings incoming leads into a visible workflow, scores their priority, and supports follow-up based on that score.

The solution
